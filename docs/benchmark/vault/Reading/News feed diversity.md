# News feed diversity

2,400 volunteers, twelve weeks, on a platform whose ranking model was trained on clicks and dwell time, with no term for diversity.

- The median user went from 6.3 sections a week to 4.1 by week twelve.
- Readers sorted into clusters: only 3 percent of the links in the co-reading graph joined different clusters.
- Fix tested in a follow-up arm: every tenth slot shows one random story from a section the reader rarely opens. That brought back most of the lost diversity for about 2 percent of clicks.
