# Street trees and block heat

Thermal survey plus leaf-on canopy maps for 96 blocks in central Larkhaven.

- Every ten extra percentage points of canopy: surface about 1.9 °C cooler. Canopy alone explains 70 percent of the variation between blocks.
- The flight was on a cloudless afternoon in late July, 14:40 to 15:25, after four days without rain.
- Blocks touching Millrace Park sat about 2.0 degrees below the fitted line; one street further out, no difference at all.
- The hottest blocks, by the rail yard and the bus depot, had flat bitumen roofs and almost no trees.
- A young street tree needs fifteen to twenty years before its crown shades much of the pavement.
