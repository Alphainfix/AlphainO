# Sourdough fermentation temperature

One starter, identical wheat doughs fermented at 18, 24 and 30 °C.

- At 30 °C the dough doubled in just under four hours; at 18 °C it took nine.
- Tasters called the warm loaves mild and a little yeasty; the cool ferment was sharper, almost vinegary.
- Big irregular holes formed only under the top crust, where gas gathered late in the bake.
- 24 °C was the best compromise between speed and flavour.
- The rye starter was kept at 100 percent hydration and fed twice a day for two weeks before the trials.
