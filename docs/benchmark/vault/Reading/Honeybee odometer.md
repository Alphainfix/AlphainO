# Honeybee odometer

A demonstration paper on how a forager judges how far it has flown. Bees were trained to feeders 50 to 400 m from the hive across a meadow, and through short tunnels lined with patterns.

## What they found

- Over the open meadow the waggle run grows at a steady rate: roughly 95 ms longer for every extra 100 m.
- After only 8 m inside the checkerboard tunnel, the bees danced as though the trip had been about 210 m.
- With axial stripes on the tunnel walls the dance barely moved, because stripes along the flight line give almost no image motion.
- Conclusion: the odometer adds up image motion across the eye, not the energy a flight costs.

## Open questions

- Would a headwind (more effort, same scenery) leave the dance unchanged? The paper does not test it.
- Bumblebees do not dance, so the trick would need another readout.
