# Garden log

- 12 April: planted lavender and borage along the fence for the bees.
- 3 May: first bumblebees on the borage.
- 20 June: the neighbour's hive swarmed into our apple tree; the club came to collect it.
- 9 September: the ivy on the back wall is in flower, full of wasps and hoverflies.
