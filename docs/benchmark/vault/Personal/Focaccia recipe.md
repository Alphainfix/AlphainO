# Focaccia

- 500 g bread flour, 400 g water, 10 g salt, 4 g instant yeast, plenty of olive oil.
- Mix, rest 30 minutes, then stretch and fold three times.
- Let it rise in the oiled tin until doubled, about two hours in a warm kitchen.
- Dimple, add rosemary and flaky salt, bake at 230 °C for 22 minutes.
