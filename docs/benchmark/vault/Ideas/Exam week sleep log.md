# Exam week sleep log

After [[Sleep spindles and memory]]: log my own nights during the January exams.

- Learn 40 Finnish words each evening at 21:30, test myself at 7:30 the next day.
- Lights out by 23:00 on four nights, by 1:00 on the other three, and compare.
- A cheap sleep tracker cannot count spindles, so I can only compare short and long nights.
- Stop if it starts eating into revision time.
