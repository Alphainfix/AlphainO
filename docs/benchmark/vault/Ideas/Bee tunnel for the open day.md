# Bee tunnel for the open day

Rebuild the patterned-tunnel trick from [[Honeybee odometer]] for the department open day, together with the beekeeping club.

- A 6 m plywood corridor lined with a printed checkerboard of 10 cm squares, with the feeder at the far end.
- The observation hive goes on the roof garden, so visitors can watch the dances on a screen.
- If the paper holds, our bees should report a trip of a couple of hundred metres.
- Cost: about £180 for plywood and printing; Mira lends the observation hive.
- Risk: once September ends the colony may prefer flowering ivy to the feeder, so run it early in the month.
