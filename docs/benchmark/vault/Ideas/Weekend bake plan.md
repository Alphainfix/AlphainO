# Weekend bake plan

Using the 24 °C sweet spot from [[Sourdough fermentation temperature]].

- Friday 22:00: feed the starter.
- Saturday 9:00 mix, bulk in the proofing box at 24 °C, shape around 14:00.
- Cold retard in the fridge overnight, bake Sunday morning at 250 °C in the Dutch oven.
- If the kitchen is cold, put the box on the lowest shelf near the radiator.
