# Shady walking routes

A summer map of walking routes that stay in the shade, after [[Street trees and block heat]].

- Use the city's open canopy layer (2023) and walk the candidate streets at 15:00 on a hot day.
- Route A: station to the library along Weir Street and the Millrace Park edge, about 1.4 km, mostly shaded.
- Avoid the stretch past the bus depot: no trees at all.
- Share it on the neighbourhood forum before next June.
